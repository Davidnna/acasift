# AcaSift brand guide

## Brand
- Product name: AcaSift
- Pronunciation: AY-kah-sift
- Tagline: Find the overlap. Follow the source.
- Product descriptor: Academic similarity analysis
- Positioning: A practical academic document comparison and source-review platform.

## Visual identity
- Midnight Navy: `#14243D` — primary brand and structural colour.
- Teal: `#18A999` — primary action and accent colour.
- Gold: `#DFA64A` — match and review highlight.
- Cloud White: `#F5F7FB` — application background.
- Typography: Inter, with system sans-serif fallbacks.
- Mark: document page with a magnifying-glass motif.

## Product language
Prefer “similarity analysis”, “matching passages”, “reference source” and “source review”.
Do not describe a similarity score as a percentage of plagiarism or claim that the system
proves academic misconduct. A human reviewer must interpret the result in context.

## Academic project relationship
AcaSift is the software brand. The approved final-year project title remains:
“Design and Implementation of a Plagiarism Detection System.”
