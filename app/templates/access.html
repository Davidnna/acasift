Your AcaSift workspace
starts with an account.

AcaSift currently uses administrator-managed accounts to control access to the document analysis workspace.

01

Request access from your institution

If your institution or department operates AcaSift, contact its authorised system administrator and request an account.

02

Receive your sign-in details

The administrator creates your account and provides the credentials or instructions you need to sign in.

03

Once your account is active, sign in to access the document workspace and available comparison features.
