IMPORTANT TO UNDERSTAND

A similarity score is a signal, not a verdict.

Text overlap can occur in properly cited quotations, references, standard terminology and commonly used phrases. AcaSift provides indicators and matching passages for human assessment. It does not independently determine whether plagiarism has occurred.
